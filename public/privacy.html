What we collect

The contact form on this site does not submit to a server. When you fill it in and hit send, your browser opens your own email app with a message pre-filled and addressed to us — you choose whether to actually send it, and it goes directly from your inbox to ours. We never receive, store, or process anything you type into that form unless you send that email yourself.

If you email or WhatsApp us directly, we keep that conversation the way anyone would — in our inbox or chat history — only to reply to you and follow up on the enquiry. We don't sell, share, or use it for anything else.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any third-party tracking script. Nothing about your visit is recorded.
