Common questions

Is the mock really free?

Yes. We build you a working page based on your brief and send you the link. If you don't want to go ahead after seeing it, that's the end of it — no invoice, no obligation.

How long does the mock take?

Usually 2–3 days from the time we've understood your brief. The full build depends on scope — a single-page site is about a week, a larger site two to three.

Do I need to buy the domain and hosting myself?

You can, and we'll help you pick. Or we'll set it all up and hand you the logins. Either way the accounts are in your name — you own everything.

Can I update the site myself afterwards?

Yes. We hand over a short walkthrough of how to change text, images and prices. Anything bigger, message us — or take the Care plan and we handle it.
