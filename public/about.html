How we work

For websites, we build a real, working mock before any money changes hands — not a PDF, not a template preview. You decide whether to continue after seeing it, with zero advance owed either way. Automations and custom products are scoped on a call instead, since a workflow can't be judged in a five-minute browser preview the way a page can — you get the feature list and the price in writing before anything is built.

For Standard Fireworks, that meant a full six-page site — live price calculator, gallery, FAQ — tested across five screen sizes, before a single rupee changed hands. Only once they'd seen it and said yes did we move to the real build.

From there we build for keeps, not just for launch. The tools we reach for are the same ones production teams use — Next.js, Cloudflare Workers, D1 — chosen because they're fast, cheap to run, and don't lock you into a bill that outlives the season. Standard Fireworks now runs on Cloudflare's free tier: effectively ₹0/month hosting, which matters for a business that's dormant ten months and slammed before Deepavali.

Security isn't an afterthought either — during hardening on that same project we caught and fixed a stored-XSS hole in the admin panel and a forgeable session cookie, the kind of thing that doesn't show up until someone actually tests for it.

And when we're done, it's yours. No lock-in, no waiting on us to change a price or swap a photo — you get an admin panel that puts you in control, and the option to walk away owing nothing if you're not happy with what we showed you.
